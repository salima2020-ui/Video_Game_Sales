## Insights related with this project

**Note (Regional Scope):** The regional analysis focuses on three key gaming markets: **North America (NA)**, **Europe (EU)**, and **Japan (JP)**. Percentage distributions reflect each market's contribution to total global sales.
**Note (Data Range):** To ensure data accuracy and avoid incomplete figures from recent releases, the dataset covers games released between **1980 and 2016**.
**Note (Metric Units):** All sales metrics across charts and summaries are represented in **millions of units sold**.
**Top Performing Genres:** **Action** leads global sales with **1.75B units**, followed by **Sports (1.33B)** and **Shooter (1.05B)**.
**Dominant Publisher:** **Nintendo** ranks #1 overall with a total of **1.78B global sales**.
**Regional Breakdown:** **North America (NA)** accounts for **49%** of global sales, **Europe (EU)** represents **27%**, and **Japan (JP)** makes up **14%**.
**Top Platforms:** **PS2** is the best-selling platform with **1.25B units**, followed by **X360 (969M)** and **PS3 (939M)**.
**Peak Industry Year:** Video game sales hit an all-time high in **2008**, reaching **678M units sold**.
**Best-Selling Game:** **Wii Sports** claims the top spot with **82.7M copies sold**.
